About NLYSAUHI

Full Name: Ngok Lual Yak Students' Association at Universities and Higher Institutes (NLYSAUHI)

Location: Konyokonyo Market, Juba, South Sudan
Type: Non-Profit, Non-Political, Student Community Organization
Motto: Education is Light

Who We Are:
NLYSAUHI brings together all sons and daughters of Ngok Lual Yak who are studying in various universities and higher institutes across South Sudan and abroad. We were formed to unite, support, and empower our students academically, socially, and culturally.

Objectives:
• Foster unity and brotherhood among Ngok Lual Yak students
• Provide academic support, tutoring, and mentorship
• Support welfare of needy students
• Preserve and promote Ngok Lual Yak culture
• Represent students in community and national affairs
• Guide secondary school leavers to university
• Organize sports, debates, and cultural events
• Build connection with elders and community leaders

Membership: Open to all Ngok Lual Yak students in universities, colleges, and higher institutes. Secondary students preparing for university are associate members.

Why Education is Light? Because education removes darkness of ignorance, poverty, and disunity. Light guides our community forward.

History, Mission & Vision

📜 History: NLYSAUHI was formed by early Ngok Lual Yak students in Juba who saw need for unity away from home. From small meetings under trees, it grew to 3000+ Members across all universities. Our motto is Education is Light.

🎯 Mission: To foster unity, promote education, support welfare, and empower Ngok Lual Yak students to become future leaders.

👁️ Vision: An educated, united, self-reliant Ngok Lual Yak community where every student succeeds and gives back.

💎 Core Values: Unity, Education, Service, Respect, Integrity, Culture.

📋 Website Rules & Constitution

1. Approval System

All news and photos MUST be approved by admin before appearing on public website. No auto-posting.

2. Content Rules

✅ Allowed: Education news, association events, sports, culture, student achievements, official announcements.
❌ NOT Allowed: Politics, tribal hate, insults, fake news, pornography, violence, personal attacks.

3. Photo Rules

• Photos must be clear, respectful, related to NLYSAUHI.
• No edited fake photos.
• Must have caption and owner name.
• Max 5 photos per person per week.

4. News Rules

• Title must be short and true.
• Description must be factual.
• Must include real photo (no stolen internet photos without source).
• Author must use real name.

5. Membership Rules

• Members must respect all leaders and members.
• Must attend meetings when possible.
• No discrimination based on gender, clan, or university.
• Membership can be revoked for misconduct.

6. Admin Powers

• Admin (Chairperson & Info Sec) can Approve, Reject, or Delete any content.
• Admin login is secret: only minyielmakuach@gmail.com
• Admin must check facts before approving.

7. Privacy

No sharing of phone numbers or private data of members without permission. Use official contacts only: 0921 300 670 / 0920 616 316

Breaking rules = content deleted + warning. Repeat = banned from submitting.
